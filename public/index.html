WebMCP · 原生页面工具WebMCP · native page tools

Agent 负责跑腿，你负责拍板。Your agent does the legwork. You make the call.

登录后，TapeNow 会在页面上注册一组 WebMCP 工具。你的 Agent 直接用它们操作工作台，不用抓网页，也拿不到你的密钥。Once you sign in, TapeNow registers WebMCP tools on the page. Your agent drives the dashboard through them — no scraping, and never your keys.

  1. 01
    发现Discover

    Agent 读取你当前角色能用的工具。The agent sees exactly the tools your role allows.

  2. 02
    准备Prepare

    生成操作单，绑定文件、目标、钱包和预算。It drafts a request pinned to files, target, wallet and budget.

  3. 03
    你批准You approve

    在你自己的界面里核对差异，点批准。You review the diff in your own interface and approve.

  4. 04
    执行Execute

    只执行你批准的那一张；结果不明不会自动重发。Only what you approved runs; unclear results are never resent.

费用Fees

钱付给谁，写得清清楚楚。Know exactly who you pay.

公测期间免费Free during beta

使用 TapeNow 不收费。每个账号包含 3 个项目、9 个版本和 100 MiB 归档空间。TapeNow itself costs nothing. Each account includes 3 projects, 9 versions and 100 MiB of archive space.

上链费用直付Onchain fees, paid direct

gas 付给区块链网络；流片、开通容器、激活地址按上游合约定价，由你的钱包直接支付。TapeNow 不代收、不加价。Gas goes to the network; chip, container and address activation are priced by upstream contracts and paid straight from your wallet. TapeNow neither collects nor marks them up.

访客零成本Free for visitors

访客打开网站不需要钱包，也不用为每次访问付 gas。你自己的云服务按各自套餐计费。Visitors need no wallet and pay no gas to open your site. Your own cloud services bill you on their own plans.
