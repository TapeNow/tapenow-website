# Security policy · 安全政策

**English.** Please do not open public issues for security problems. Report them privately through GitHub: on this repository, open **Security → Report a vulnerability**. Include the affected URL, the steps to reproduce, and the impact. We will acknowledge your report and keep you updated. This covers tapenow.dev and the TapeNow workbench at app.tapenow.dev.

Out of scope: denial-of-service, automated scanner output without a working proof of concept, and social engineering.

**中文。** 请不要通过公开 Issue 报告安全问题。请在本仓库的 **Security → Report a vulnerability** 中私下提交，写明受影响的网址、复现步骤和影响范围。我们会确认收到，并告知处理进展。适用范围包括 tapenow.dev 和 app.tapenow.dev 上的 TapeNow 工作台。

不在范围内：拒绝服务攻击、没有可行验证的自动扫描结果，以及社会工程。
